## EU harmonisation findings

The European Commission explains that use of harmonised standards is voluntary and that an OJ citation supports presumption only for the legal requirements covered. Record the cited edition and coverage instead of labeling every IEC document “EU compliant.” [Commission source](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-topics-interest/harmonised-standards_en).

Decision (EU) 2026/1231 adds EN 60601-1:2006 with EN 60601-1:2006/A13:2024 to the MDR list. This is an OJ citation finding, not a conclusion that the uploaded AAMI copy is equivalent or that other technical amendments can be ignored. Its 22 June 2026 corrigendum changes the effective date of Annex point (5), not that general-standard entry. [Decision](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2026/1231/oj), [corrigendum](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32026D1231R%2801%29).

EN ISO 14971:2019 with A11:2021 was cited for the European medical-device regulations; the supplied international text alone does not provide the European mapping. [ISO committee notice](https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc210/home/news/content-left-area/news-and-updates/recognition-of-en-iso-14971-as-a.html).

Recheck the Commission's current MDR list and exact EN package at baseline approval and release. No harmonised-status conclusion is made here for 62304, 81001-5-1 or 60601-2-16.
